Revision

What's changed since March

The first version of this proposal was built around Grant's outreach. After the April 22 session with PJ, two things became clear. PJ wants visible inclusion: this engagement should serve his foundation pipeline alongside Grant's verticals. And PJ has already built a precise Sales Navigator playbook with Intero Advisory, covering personas, Boolean search variations, a 990-driven research workflow, and an ICP scoring rubric. The opportunity is to operationalise that playbook on a weekly cadence, not redesign it.

This revision splits the engagement into two parallel tracks inside the same $1,000 / month budget. Grant's outreach volume comes down to make room. PJ keeps full ownership of his outreach. AI handles the pre-funnel research and mutual contact mapping he currently does by hand.

Overview

What this is

Your existing engagement ($750 / month) handles visibility: LinkedIn posts, blog content, newsletter. That keeps running as-is.

This proposal adds an active layer on top of that foundation. Two tracks, opposite operating philosophies, shared budget. Track A automates the outreach Grant doesn't have time to run. Track B clears the runway for the warm intros PJ already runs himself.

Track A

Grant's verticals. Linda runs scripted outreach from Grant's LinkedIn account across two test verticals. Grant only engages warm conversations.

Track B

PJ's foundation pipeline. AI scrubs PJ's saved searches, maps mutual contacts and COIs, and scores prospects against PJ's ICP rubric. PJ runs the warm intros himself.

Why two tracks, not one merged workflow. Grant's track is automation of human outreach. PJ's track is precision research with no outreach automation. Same budget, different mechanics. Keeping them visibly separate avoids muddying either one.
Track A

Outreach Engine for Grant

Niche testing, not niche guessing

Rather than betting six months on a single vertical, we test two niches in parallel. The planning session with Colin, Grant, and PJ identifies the two strongest verticals to target. Outreach runs across both, and volume shifts toward the niche producing the best acceptance rates and conversations.

Prospect targeting

10 to 15 deeply researched target companies per month, split across the two niches. Each prospect is profiled with key contacts, recent activity, and potential pain points. Personalised connection messages drafted for each.

Quality over quantity. The first version had a second tier of LinkedIn-search prospects (30 to 40 per month). That tier is removed in this revision to fund Track B inside the same budget.

LinkedIn outreach playbook

  • Connection request messages tailored by prospect type, with multiple variants to keep it natural
  • Post-connection DM sequence that builds rapport over weeks before suggesting a virtual coffee
  • Liking and commenting on prospects' posts to build visibility before and during the outreach sequence
  • Comment bank categorised by post type so Linda always has the right response ready
  • All written in Grant's voice, referencing ESP's real client results where relevant

What runs each week

  • 8 to 10 targeted connection requests from Grant's LinkedIn
  • Like and comment on prospect posts to build visibility and rapport
  • Follow up with new connections via the DM sequence
  • Follow-up DMs to connections from previous weeks
  • Flag warm responses for Grant
  • All activity logged in the tracking spreadsheet

What Grant does

  • Shows up for warm conversations when they are flagged
  • 30 minutes monthly with Colin to see the numbers and approve any changes

Nothing else. The hunting is handled.

Track B

Pre-Funnel Research Engine for PJ

This track operationalises the playbook PJ has already built with Intero Advisory ("PJ LinkedIn Sales Navigator Activation," April 2026). Personas, Boolean search variations, the 990-based AI research workflow, and the ICP scoring rubric are PJ's. We deliver them on a weekly cadence so the prep work stops eating his calendar.

Weekly inputs

  • PJ's saved Sales Navigator searches (the foundation pipeline he already curates)
  • PJ's COI account list, with introduction-willingness flagged

Weekly outputs, per prospect

  • ProPublica 990 extract: total assets, total revenue, grants paid, officer compensation, named contractors
  • Website team-page scrape: leadership titles, technology and data signals, named software platforms
  • ICP scoring against PJ's JSON rubric: organisational scale, operational complexity, external spend signals, data and evaluation needs, tech gap, buyer readiness, constraints. Final fit_score and priority bucket (high 80 to 100, medium 60 to 79, low 0 to 59), plus a recommended outreach hook citing one specific numeric fact
  • Mutual contact list with COI matches surfaced and ordered by introduction-willingness

Weekly delivery

One Google Doc each Friday containing 8 scored prospects with their COI map, ICP score, and recommended hook. PJ scans it, decides which warm intros to chase, and runs the outreach himself.

What PJ does

  • Reviews the Friday Doc and decides which warm intros to pursue
  • Runs his own outreach from his own account, on his own cadence
  • Optional 30 minutes monthly to refine the ICP signals based on real outputs

PJ owns his outreach. We just clear the runway.

Cadence

How the week runs

  • Monday to Wednesday. Linda runs Track A outreach: connection requests, DM sequences, post engagement on Grant's account.
  • Wednesday to Thursday. Linda and AI produce the Track B research package: 8 prospects scored, COI map drafted, hooks written.
  • Friday. PJ Google Doc delivered. Grant tracker updated with the week's activity.
  • Monthly. 30 minutes with Colin covering both tracks. Optional 30 minutes with PJ on ICP signal quality.

Clarity

What this is not

Not a replacement for existing content.

The $750 engagement keeps running. This is a separate layer focused on outreach and pre-funnel research.

Not automation of PJ's outreach.

Track B stops at the research handoff. PJ owns the warm-intro outreach, on his account, on his cadence.

Not cold spam on Grant's side.

Connection requests are targeted and personalised. DMs build rapport before asking for anything. Likes and comments are part of building rapport, not noise.

Not PJ's network handed to Linda.

PJ's COI list and saved searches inform Track B research. Outreach to those contacts stays with PJ.
